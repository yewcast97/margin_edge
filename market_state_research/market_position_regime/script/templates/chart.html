VIX · daily

The percentile is on a fixed 0–100 scale with a line at 50; each rests on the closes of the 252 XNYS sessions ending with the session, from Cboe's daily history of the index. A line breaks where a session has no value: a session not fetched, or one without a close on every one of the 252 sessions.
